# Visual Analytics & Dashboards

The computational outputs from our spatial pipelines are integrated into interactive web portals and business intelligence dashboards for policy-level decision-making.

## Walkable Southeast Asia
A comprehensive web portal detailing the comparative walkability, livability, and accessibility metrics across Jakarta, Manila, Ho Chi Minh City, and Phnom Penh.
* **URL:** `https://ddum-sutd.github.io/walkable_southeastasia-main/`

## North America Micro-Spatial Interventions (Tableau Public)
Interactive dashboards translating the unsupervised Machine Learning outputs (PCA and K-Means clustering) into block-level prescriptive livability interventions for Mexican and US metropolitan areas.
* **Monterrey & Cancun:** `https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/david.kreutz/viz/TableauDashboardClaude/Dashboard1`
